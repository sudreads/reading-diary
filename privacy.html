Reading Diary - privacy

Plain-language version. Last updated 27 October 2026. Reading Diary is an invite-only beta run by one person.

Private - only you

  • Your notebook pages, drawings, comments, lessons and reading log.
  • Your diary is stored on your phone and in your reader account (Google Firebase), where the security rules let only your own account read it. Drawings are backed up to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only this app can open.
  • Honest caveat: the person running the app owns the Firebase project, so Google's console could technically show the stored diary text. It is never looked at, shared or sold.
  • Ratings and reviews on books where "Post publicly" is off.

Friends only - people you both accepted

  • Your reader name, your current streak, how many books you've finished, the book you're reading now, and the last book you finished with its star rating.
  • Never your notebook, drawings, comments or lessons.
  • Unfriend anyone from Settings → Friends; they stop seeing this straight away.

Public - every signed-in beta member

  • Your reader name (@handle).
  • For books you finish with "Post publicly" on: your star rating, your review text, which book it is, and the exact time you posted or edited it (the app shows month and year, but the exact time is in the data).
  • Your email address and your Google profile are never shown or stored in the public database.
  • Public reviews are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), run by Google.

Community rules

  • Review the book, not people. No insults, hate, or personal information about anyone.
  • Warn before big spoilers - better, leave them out.
  • No ads or spam. Don't use your full real name as your reader name.
  • Tap Report on any review that breaks these rules. The admin can delete any review.

Deleting your data

  • Turn off "Post publicly" on a book, or delete your review from its Reviews page, to remove that review.
  • Settings → Delete my public data removes all your public reviews, your reports, your friendships and friend requests, the reading card your friends see, and your reviews sign-in. Your reader name stays reserved so nobody else can use it to pretend to be you.
  • Your private diary is yours: Settings → Download backup gives you a copy; uninstalling the app and deleting the app's data in Google Drive (Drive → Settings → Manage apps) removes it.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: ask the person who invited you to the beta, or open an issue at github.com/sudreads/reading-diary.
